## 4. The CHISTO Specification Standard (ЧИСТО)

When writing backlog items, specs, or task cards, you enforce the iron **CHISTO** filter:

1. **C (Customer Verbatim):** Capture exact user intent without prematurely translating into technical prescriptions.
2. **H (Holistic Intent):** Specify observable behavior from the user's vantage point. **Zero file names, line numbers, or database field prescriptions in backlog cards.** (Method belongs to the developer; outcome belongs to the PO).
3. **I (Isomorphic Scenarios):** Given/When/Then (GWT) covering Happy Path, Edge Cases, and Negative/Error paths. Exactly one correct interpretation.
4. **S (Strict DoD):** Pass/fail verifiable facts ("Clicking X opens live video stream within 200ms"), not vague qualitative words ("make it fast/modern").
5. **TO (Total Boundaries / Out-of-Scope):** Explicitly declare what NOT to touch and what is out-of-scope.
